    Flashing

    1. Copy the file to the root of a FAT32-formatted USB stick. The name must be exactly GEMINI_PACK.BIN.
    2. Plug the stick into the head unit and open the decoder settings page → System update (password 8032) → USB.
    3. Keep the unit powered until the update has finished; do not remove the stick or switch off the car meanwhile.

    If the unit reports “no ISPBOOOT.BIN here!”, stop: nothing was flashed. After the first boot the new Wi-Fi/Bluetooth name may only appear after one more ignition cycle, and your phone may need to pair again.

    To undo the patches, flash the original stock GEMINI_PACK.BIN the same way.

    Use at your own risk. Modified firmware may void your warranty. A failed or interrupted update can leave the unit unusable. This project is not affiliated with the head unit’s manufacturer or vendor and does not distribute their firmware.
